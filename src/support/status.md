## During an incident

1. Check whether the problem is on your side: retry a simple request, such as
   [`GET /v1/models`](../api/models.md), from another machine or network.
2. [Contact support](contact.md) with your request IDs (the `x-request-id`
   response header) and timestamps in UTC.
